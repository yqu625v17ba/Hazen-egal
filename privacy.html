HAZEN™ Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

This policy explains how HAZEN™ (“the Bot”) may handle information when used in Discord servers. Please verify this text against the Bot’s actual code, database, logs, and hosting setup before publishing. Do not claim data is collected or deleted in a way that is not true.

1. Information that may be processed

Depending on which features are enabled and how the Bot is implemented, HAZEN™ may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel and role IDs, command inputs, moderation settings or action records, leveling/economy balances and activity counters, giveaway entries, and welcome/auto-role configuration. The Bot may receive message content only where needed for a command or a feature that is enabled and permitted by Discord.

2. How information is used

Information is used to operate commands and features; apply server configuration; maintain levels, virtual balances, and giveaway participation; perform moderation requested by authorized server staff; troubleshoot issues; and protect the Bot against abuse or technical problems.

3. Storage, sharing, and service providers

Data may be stored in the Bot’s database or hosting environment for as long as needed to provide its features. Hosting, database, music, or other providers may process data as necessary to provide their services. HAZEN™ does not sell personal information. Data may also be disclosed when required by law or to address security, abuse, or service integrity concerns.

4. Retention and deletion requests

Retention depends on the feature and the Bot’s actual implementation. Server administrators or users may request deletion of data associated with their server or Discord user ID through the Bot’s official support contact. Requests will be reviewed and handled where technically possible and legally required. Server removal alone may not automatically erase all stored records unless the Bot is designed to do so.

5. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information, but no online service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Children and eligibility

HAZEN™ is intended for people who are allowed to use Discord under Discord’s age requirements and applicable law. Do not use the Bot if you are not eligible to use Discord.

7. Third-party services

HAZEN™ operates on Discord and may rely on third-party services. Their own privacy policies and terms apply to their services. HAZEN™ does not control those third-party practices.

8. Policy changes

This policy may be updated when the Bot’s features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when changes are published.
